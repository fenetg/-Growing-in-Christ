Our Church Programs

At Growing in Christ Seventh-day Adventist Church, we organize different programs to strengthen our faith, develop our spiritual lives, and bring our church family together. Through worship, Bible study, prayer, music, and fellowship, we learn more about God's Word and share His love with others. Our programs welcome children, youth, adults, and visitors to grow together in Christ.

Sabbath School

Sabbath School is a special time for Bible study, discussion, and spiritual learning. It helps church members understand God's Word and grow in faith

Divine Service

Divine Service is a special time when church members come together to worship and praise God. It includes opening prayers, Bible readings, singing, special music, and a sermon based on God's Word. Through this service, we strengthen our faith, learn spiritual lessons, and grow closer to Jesus Christ.

Youth Ministry

Youth Ministry is designed to help young people grow in their relationship with Jesus Christ. Through Bible study, prayer, fellowship, worship, and different activities, young people develop their faith, discover their talents, build leadership skills, and encourage one another to live according to God's Word.

Children's Ministry

Children's Ministry focuses on teaching children about God's love and helping them develop a strong foundation of faith in Jesus Christ. Through Bible stories, songs, prayer, fun activities, and interactive lessons, children learn Christian values and how to follow God's Word in their daily lives. Our goal is to help every child grow spiritually in a loving and welcoming church environment.

Pathfinder Club

The Pathfinder Club is a worldwide Seventh-day Adventist youth organization that helps children and teenagers grow physically, mentally, socially, and spiritually. Through Bible study, camping, community service, teamwork, outdoor activities, and practical skills, Pathfinders learn leadership, discipline, responsibility, and love for others. Its mission is to help young people develop a strong relationship with Jesus Christ and become responsible members of their communities.

Prayer Meeting

Prayer Meeting is a special gathering where church members come together to communicate with God through prayer and study His Word. It provides an opportunity to share testimonies, pray for one another, discuss spiritual lessons, and strengthen our faith. Through these meetings, we build a closer relationship with God and encourage one another in our Christian journey.

Choir Ministry

Choir Ministry is dedicated to praising and glorifying God through singing and music. Choir members use their voices and musical talents to lead the congregation in worship, share messages of faith, and inspire others through spiritual songs. Through regular practice, teamwork, and dedication, the choir helps create a meaningful worship experience and spreads God's love through music.
