Church Name

Growing in Christ Seventh-day Adventist Church

About our church

Our History

Growing in Christ Seventh-day Adventist Church is a Christian community founded on the teachings of the Holy Bible. Our church follows the Seventh-day Adventist faith, sharing the message of God's love, salvation through Jesus Christ, and the hope of His second coming. We continue to grow through worship, prayer, Bible study, fellowship, and service to our community.

Our Mission

Our mission is to share the love of God, preach the everlasting gospel, and help people grow in their relationship with Jesus Christ. We are committed to worshiping God, studying the Bible, serving others, and spreading the message of hope and salvation to the world.

Our Beliefs

We believe in one God: the Father, the Son, and the Holy Spirit. We believe that the Bible is the Word of God and that salvation comes through faith in Jesus Christ. We observe the seventh-day Sabbath (Saturday) as a day of worship and rest. We also believe in the second coming of Jesus Christ and the promise of eternal life.

Our Community

We are a loving church family that welcomes people of all ages. Through fellowship, prayer, Bible study, and community service, we encourage one another to grow in faith and love. We support children, youth, families, and everyone who wants to know more about Jesus Christ. Our goal is to share God's love and make a positive difference in our community.
